# Dr. Gülsüm Meço
### Researcher in AI-Supported Education, Computer Vision & Educational Technology

Welcome to my research portfolio. I work at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, education, and technology integration**, with an emphasis on translating research into practical learning experiences.

- **Postdoctoral research:** Boğaziçi University — AUTIM project, supported by the TÜBİTAK 2218 National Postdoctoral Research Fellowship Programme
- **Teaching:** Part-time Lecturer, Faculty of Education, İstanbul 29 Mayıs University
- **Current studies:** PhD student in Computer and Information Systems Engineering, Sakarya University
- **Education:** PhD in Science Education, Yıldız Technical University

## Research Interests

- Artificial intelligence in education and AI-supported science learning
- Computer vision, object detection, and image segmentation
- Educational technology integration and teacher professional development
- STEM education, Arduino-based learning, and digital learning environments
- Technology, pedagogy, and content knowledge (TPACK)

## Selected Research & Projects

### NLP-assisted document analysis in science education
Gülsüm Meço’s study of *Twenty Thousand Leagues under the Sea* follows 1,680 paragraphs from keyword screening to contextual review and curriculum matching. [Read the full paper, explore the original workbooks and run the code](projects/verne-science-education/).

### AUTIM — Adaptable Unit-Based Technology Integration Model
A research project developing an adaptable, unit-based approach to integrating educational technologies into teaching. Conducted at **Boğaziçi University** with support from **TÜBİTAK 2218**.

### AI-Enhanced Science Learning
Research on real-time human-organ recognition using **YOLOv8** to support science education.

### Computer Vision & Segmentation
Ongoing exploration of deep-learning methods, including **SegFormer** and **U-Net**, for image-segmentation workflows.

### Arduino-Supported STEM Learning
Research and learning activities connecting biology, science, engineering, and technology through Arduino-based applications.
